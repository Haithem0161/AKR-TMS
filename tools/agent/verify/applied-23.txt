### 7.N `feat.feature_runs` is purged by row (V-23-06-a, MEDIUM; DR-413)
Lead decision. Phase 13 does not partition `feat.feature_runs` (one row per month and feature), so a partition drop could never run. The phase 13 assignment row takes the clock `age`.
