### 7.N The workflow subjects of kyc_review and exit_decision (V-15-06-a, V-15-01-b, HIGH; DR-413)
This phase registers one `WorkflowSubject` (phase 04) for each of its two resource kinds. Neither declares parties; `check_before_decision` keeps its default.
- `KycReviewWorkflowSubject`: `resource_kind` `kyc_review`, `attribute_prefix` `review`, attributes `kind` (text) and `level_at_open` (integer). `start_context` freezes both, sets `maker_id` to the reviewer, and sets `classification` to `restricted` when the party's tier is `restricted` or `suspicion`, else `internal`. `visible_to` and `visibility_sql` apply the masking of section 3.6 to the review's party. `on_ended` changes nothing: the review completes only through `record_decision` (section 3.4 step 7).
- `ExitDecisionWorkflowSubject`: `resource_kind` `exit_decision`, `attribute_prefix` `exit`, no attributes. `start_context` freezes `review_id` (the completed review whose outcome `exit_candidate` opened the instance, section 3.4 step 7 sub-step 6) and `party_id`, sets `maker_id` to that review's reviewer and `classification` to `restricted`. `visible_to` and `visibility_sql`: role M only, as the exit register. `on_ended` changes nothing.
Section 3.4 step 8: the `review_id` of the `kyc.exit_register` row is read from the instance context, never from the body; `NewExitEntry` keeps its fields.

### 7.N The country fan-out: page query, effective date and trigger (V-15-02-b, V-15-02-c, V-15-02-d, MEDIUM; DR-413)
Section 3.3 row 2.
- Section 2, the port `PartyFactsSource` gains `async fn parties_for_country(&self, conn: &mut PgConnection, iso2: &str, page: Keyset) -> Result<Page<Uuid>, RepoError>;`. It returns one keyset page of party ids in id order, sized by `rating.country_fanout_page`: parties with a current row (`recorded_to IS NULL`) whose `nationality` or `residence_country` is `iso2`, and the `owned_party_id` of each open `kyc.ownership_inputs` row (`valid_to IS NULL`) whose `owner_party_id` is a party with `residence_country` equal to `iso2`.

- The topic `ref.country_level_changed` is the DSL event `country_table.changed`, the `on` value of TR-10 (DSL.md C.5). TR-10's action is `rescore`, and the fan-out is that action: it opens no review and does not run the trigger path of section 7.7.
