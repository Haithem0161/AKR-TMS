+### 7.19 Known-answer cases the bank adds (owner decision 2; DR-415)
+A bank adds its own known-answer cases through change control: a name, with identifiers when the bank has them, that must hit a named list entry (`hit`) or must not hit it (`no_hit`). This is the bank-side intake of N-44: a spelling the analysts want kept as a known hit, and a name that matched wrongly kept as a hard negative. The shipped corpus stays synthetic (N-38) and it alone decides the gate. The bank's cases run in every gate and oracle run and are reported beside it. They never block a list activation, because a list update is a legal duty (the 12-hour rule) and a case can go stale when the publisher changes an entry. A configuration change that would lose a known hit says so before it is approved (item 8). The bank-specific variant table (N-57) stays P2.
