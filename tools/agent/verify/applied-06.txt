### 7.N Required and optional fields of cbi-gap-report.v1.json (V-06-03-b, LOW; DR-413)
Section 3.5 step 4 names the keys of the document. Required: the six top-level keys `cover`, `summary`, `items`, `bank_request`, `vendor_annex` and `outcomes`, and every key that step 4 names inside them, except these optional keys: an item's `declaration` (present only when its blocking scenarios carry one; its `detail` is null when no `interim_detail` was given), and the `code` and `name` of a `linked` entry (absent for a restricted scenario, which carries `restricted: true`).
Value types: ids are UUID strings; timestamps are RFC 3339 strings; counts are integers of 0 or more; shares are numbers from 0 to 1; `title`, `text` and labels are `{ar, en}` objects of strings; `items`, `linked`, `bank_request` and `vendor_annex` are arrays. The values of `outcomes` are nullable (null until phases 17 to 21 fill them).
