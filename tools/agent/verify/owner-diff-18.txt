+### 7.28 Name rows of the party page carry `capture_source` (owner decision 9; DR-420)

+### 7.29 Copy actions for the source ids in W5 (owner decision 3; DR-416)
+Each row of the transaction explorer W5 (section 5.3) gains two actions in its row menu, "Copy account id" and "Copy counterparty id"; the second is absent when the row's `counterparty_id` is null.
