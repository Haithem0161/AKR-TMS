- Attack:
  1. Analyst A2 `x2` holds `queue.work` and the analyst network, but is not an assignee, not in the assignee pool, not on `casework.case_acl` of case C (a suspicion-tier case). `x2` has no step-up assertion.
  2. `x2` calls `GET /api/v1/cases/<C>` (phase-17.md:849 says "Suspicion tier: step-up `record_open`"). The handler loads the tier and calls `StepUpService::consume` first, so the answer is 401 `E_STEPUP_REQUIRED`.

  4. The same applies to `GET /api/v1/alerts/<A>` (phase-17.md:839, "Suspicion tier: step-up `record_open`") and to `GET /api/v1/cases/<C>/evidence/<E>/content` (phase-18.md:698-699: Cedar check, then step 2 step-up 401, with no visibility predicate before step 2).
  5. The party route is not affected: phase-18.md:866-870 orders step 1 (tier rule, 404 when refused) before step 2 (step-up 401).
- Guard search: phase-17.md:1474 only says the handler "calls `StepUpService::consume` with the class `record_open`" and gives no order. Phase-17.md:1703 tests a 401 only for `a2` (the assignee case). Grep "record_open" in phase-17.md and phase-18.md finds no sentence that runs the membership test before the step-up. The Cedar permits (phase-17.md:1474) do not use step-up. No guard found.
- Severity (FLAW only): CRITICAL for `GET /cases/{id}` and the evidence content (a person outside the case learns that a suspicion case exists); HIGH for `GET /alerts/{id}` (a suspicion-tier alert exists; a count or answer differs between masked and missing).

  On `GET /alerts/{id}`, `GET /alerts/{id}/snapshot`, `GET /cases/{id}`, `GET /cases/{id}/timeline` and `GET /cases/{id}/evidence/{evidence_id}/content`, the handler first loads the row and evaluates the phase 17 section 3.10 visibility predicate (rules 1 to 5 without the step-up clause, and phase 18 section 3.10 with the case access list).
