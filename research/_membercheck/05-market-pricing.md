# MemberCheck notes 05 — Pricing, customers, reviews, competitors
Status: partial (2026-10-11)

## Summary
- MemberCheck is an AML, KYC and sanctions screening product. Its legal entity in the Australian business register is MEMBER CHECK PTY LIMITED (ABN 64 129 012 344, Chatswood, NSW). The vendor says it "operates under Neurocom Group", and its logo reads "MemberCheck, a Nexiant brand". [PRIMARY] https://abr.business.gov.au/ABN/View?abn=64129012344 and https://membercheck.com/about-us/
- MemberCheck publishes no prices. Its pricing page is a demo request form, and its comparison table says pricing is "Based on screening volume and the features you use. Request a quote". [PRIMARY] https://membercheck.com/pricing/ and https://membercheck.com/compare/membercheck-vs-world-check/
- The sister brand NameScan trades under NEUROCOM PTY LTD. It is pay as you go, "Pay As You Go, per search", with no contract, no set-up fee and free checks. Its per-search amounts could not be retrieved. [PRIMARY] https://namescan.io/pricing and https://abr.business.gov.au/ABN/View?abn=12078580382
- The vendor says it has 16 organisations in production and one written case study, Titan Corporate Services (a Mauritius trust and company service provider with about 500 clients). No named bank customer was found. [PRIMARY] https://membercheck.com/case-studies/ and https://membercheck.com/case-studies/titan-corporate-services/
- The partner programme offers ten models, but names no reseller or distributor. The vendor names Oman as a deployment country. No Iraq link was found. [PRIMARY] https://membercheck.com/partnership/
- GetApp shows 4.8 out of 5 from 15 verified reviews. Capterra, G2, Software Advice and the TrustRadius production page returned HTTP 403, so no verbatim praise or complaint quotes were retrieved. [SECONDARY] https://web.archive.org/web/2026/https://www.getapp.com/finance-accounting-software/a/membercheck/
- The vendor compares itself with World-Check (LSEG), ComplyAdvantage, LexisNexis and First AML. No analyst or award placement for MemberCheck was found. World-Check had a 2020 partnership in Iraq with Iraqna IBTS. ### Q1. Pricing model and published prices
- MemberCheck publishes no prices. Its pricing URL is a demo request form, and it says "Request a demo for your compliance team." [PRIMARY] https://membercheck.com/pricing/
- The demo form asks for "Anticipated Annual Scan Volume" in three bands: "Less than 10,000 Scans", "10,000 to 50,000 Scans" and "More than 50,000 Scans". No price is shown for any band. [PRIMARY] https://membercheck.com/pricing/
- [INFERENCE] The volume bands probably feed a sales quote. The page does not say so, and it shows no prices. Reason: the bands sit on a sales form with no prices. https://membercheck.com/pricing/
- The vendor's comparison table gives MemberCheck's pricing as "Based on screening volume and the features you use. Request a quote". [PRIMARY] https://membercheck.com/compare/membercheck-vs-world-check/
- The vendor's table gives "Unlimited users, with no per-seat fee" as MemberCheck's user licensing. Its pitch includes "pricing that does not climb every time you add a member of staff". [PRIMARY] https://membercheck.com/compare/membercheck-vs-world-check/
- NameScan's pricing model is "Pay As You Go, per search". [PRIMARY] https://namescan.io/pricing
- NameScan says: "NameScan has no annual contracts, no minimum monthly commitment and no setup fees." [PRIMARY] https://namescan.io/pricing
- NameScan offers a trial: "A limited number of free checks are available so you can trial the platform before purchasing". [PRIMARY] https://namescan.io/pricing
- NameScan packages are "Valid 12 months from purchase." Emerald is a "PEP, sanctions and adverse media screening bundle". Sapphire is "Pay-as-you-go PEP and sanctions screening against global PEP, sanctions and law enforcement watchlists". [PRIMARY] https://namescan.io/pricing
- NameScan bills KYB and ID checks per item: "Billed per company checked." (KYB) and "Billed per check, pay as you go." (ID verification). [PRIMARY] https://namescan.io/pricing
- NameScan packages can be bought in 12 currencies: "Packages can be purchased in Australian Dollar, Canadian Dollar, Emirati Dirham, Euro, Great Britain Pound, Hong Kong Dollar, Indian Rupee, Japanese Yen, New Zealand Dollar, Singapore Dollar, South African Rand and US Dollar." [PRIMARY] https://namescan.io/pricing

- The customer count conflicts. The homepage says "13,000+", and the case-studies page says "16" organisations in production. [PRIMARY] https://membercheck.com/ and https://membercheck.com/case-studies/
