# Verification of research files 35 and 34 (MemberCheck / NameScan and Prototype for Information Technology)

Date: 2026-10-11. Verifier: independent pass, read-only on files 34 and 35 and on the working notes.

Status: finished. Both files were complete when checked (file 35: 700 lines; file 34: 549 lines, section 13 with 55 lines, and `grep -n "files 34 and 35" research/_progress.md` prints line 754). Findings: file 35 has 5 must-fix and 35 should-fix rows; file 34 has 4 must-fix and 13 should-fix rows. Row labels are identifiers, not a running count: 35-21 was withdrawn after a check against the raw copy.

## Method and limits

I read both files in full. For file 35 I traced the ten "Findings in brief", the eight rows and the count of section 10, sections 8, 9 and 11, and well over 40 factual sentences or table rows from sections 1 to 7 (about 120 in all), each against the line of the working notes 01 to 07 that the key points to, and against the agents' saved same-day copies of the pages (scratchpad src-* folders, read with grep, not fetched). For file 34 I checked every statement about the Prototype site against the lead's first-hand file, all 31 client rows, and about 80 other sentences against notes 08 to 12, files 14, 18 and 30, docs/MODULES.md, docs/plan/status.md and the saved copy of the Prototype JavaScript bundle. Machine checks (scripts kept in the scratchpad, read-only): every double-quoted string (255 in file 35, 161 in file 34) was looked up in the raw page copies and the notes; 235 of 240 quotes that carry a key sit on the page the key names (the five misses are three stat blocks that the page prints value first and label second, one quote-style difference, and one unmapped key); no quote is over 40 words; no emoji; no requirement identifier; every source key in both files is defined once and used (78 and 46 keys). I made 10 WebFetch calls: 4 for file 35 (MemberCheck Iraq page, World-Check comparison page, OpenCorporates record of MEMBER CHECK PTY LIMITED, the MemberCheck logo file) and 6 third-party addresses for file 34 (NameScan partnership page, domain registry record, LinkedIn page, Azentio page for KIIB, OpenCorporates search for the Michigan LLC, Pio-Tech references page, which returned an empty shell). I did not fetch the four pages the lead re-read, did not fetch the Prototype site, and contacted nobody. Limits: a saved page copy shows what the agents downloaded, not necessarily what a visitor sees; model-written fetch summaries can drop words; the owner's original message is not in the notes, so I could not check the owner quotes against it. What I could not check is listed at the end.
