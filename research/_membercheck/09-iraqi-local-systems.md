# MemberCheck notes 09 — Locally built Iraqi AML, screening and KYC systems
Status: partial (2026-10-11)

## Summary
- No Iraqi-built AML, sanctions-screening, blacklist or KYC system was found in the sources reached on 2026-10-11. The owner's "prototype member check" by "Mohammed" does not match any local system. No candidate fits (Q7).
- The Central Bank of Iraq (CBI) told exchange companies on 3 August 2021 to own an electronic system linked to the banned-name lists it supervises. A 1 July 2020 letter asked for internationally approved AML systems. Neither notice names a product or maker.
- The CBI issued AML and CFT controls for e-payment providers in April 2025 (board decision No. 63 of 2025), addressed to all licensed banks and e-payment companies. The PDF is scanned and its system clauses were not read.
- The AML systems seen at Iraqi customers are foreign. Pio-Tech has signed to implement AML and risk-based approach tools at Al-Tabadul (21 July 2026). ASEE, a Macedonian vendor, has signed to implement its AML solution for Al-Saqi of Baghdad (19 September 2024). Neither is Iraqi-made.
- The only new bank item is Rafidain Bank's AML and goAML activation (11 April 2025). It names no supplier. UR Islamic Bank's CapitalCompliance deal (25 November 2021) is foreign.
- The national freeze list is in decision No. 61 of 2025 of the Committee for Freezing Terrorist Funds, published in Al-Waqa'i' No. 4848 on 17 November 2025. No official name-search tool and no Iraqi firm offering list search were found. OpenSanctions carries the Iraqi list through a licensed API. It is not Iraqi-made.
- No local "name check" or "member check" service was found in Iraq.
- No founder or developer is tied to any Iraqi system. The only "Mohammed" in the freeze-list news is the Prime Minister, who is not linked to any system.
- Status is partial. ISX annual reports could not be reached. The Iraqi AML guidance PDF did not load. The "United Engineers Group" lead from research file 18 could not be confirmed.

### Q2. Systems aimed at exchange companies and e-payment firms (CBI compliance)
- [PRIMARY] Exchange companies must own an electronic system linked to the banned-name lists. CBI notice dated 3 August 2021 (page date). Quote (Arabic): "لذا يدعو هذا البنك شركاتكم المحترمة لامتلاك نظاماً إلكترونياً يتضمن الحركات اليومية لنشاطاتها مع ضرورة ربط هذا النظام مع قوائم الحرمان الدولية والمحلية التي يشرف عليها البنك المركزي العراقي". Gloss: "This bank calls on your respected companies to own an electronic system covering their daily activity, linked to the international and local banned lists that the CBI supervises." Address: https://cbi.iq/news/view/1791
- [PRIMARY] CBI letter 181/5/9 dated 1 July 2020, to exchange companies in categories A and B, confirming circular 46/5/9 of 3 February 2019. Quote (Arabic): "توفير أنظمة مكافحة غسل الأموال وتمويل الإرهاب المعتمدة عالمياً." Gloss: "Provide internationally approved AML and CFT systems." The letter also asks for an accounting system linked electronically to all branches. Address: https://cbi.iq/news/view/1513
- [PRIMARY] CBI notice dated 14 December 2021 for exchange companies in categories A and B. Quote (Arabic): "يرجى من شركات الصرافة للفئتين (A,B) اعتماد أُنموذج التقرير الفصلي الخاص بإمتثال الشركة". Gloss: "Exchange companies in categories A and B must adopt the quarterly compliance report form." This is a reporting form, not a screening system. Address: https://cbi.iq/news/view/1896
- [PRIMARY] E-payment providers: the CBI supervision page lists "ضوابط مكافحة غسل الأموال وتمويل الإرهاب لمقدمي خدمات الدفع الالكتروني" (AML and CFT controls for e-payment service providers). The cover letter, dated April 2025 and issued under board decision No. 63 of 2025, is addressed to all licensed banks and all licensed e-payment companies. It attaches the controls for implementation. The 48-page PDF is scanned and its system clauses were not read.
