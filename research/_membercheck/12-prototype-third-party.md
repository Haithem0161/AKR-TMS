# MemberCheck notes 12 — Prototype for Information Technology: independent evidence at Iraqi banks
Status: partial (2026-10-11)

## Summary

- Direct answer: no independent source confirms that any Iraqi bank uses a Prototype AML or sanctions system. Every Prototype claim is a company statement (grade B).
- Searched: 15 web searches (English and Arabic); bank, vendor and regulator pages listed under Sources; six Iraq Securities Commission PDFs; NameScan's own pages and coverage document. Details are under Not found.
- One named system has a vendor-side source: Kurdistan International Islamic Bank uses Azentio AMLOCK (vendor page, grade B). Prototype's list has "Kurdistan Islamic Bank", which may be the same bank (name match not confirmed).
- Labelled banks: no source for International Development Bank or Misk Islamic Bank. Region Trade Bank's home page names no system. The Cihan Bank and IMEIB websites show placeholder pages only. Cihan's core is Path iMAL (2012 search snippet); no AML vendor is named for Cihan.
- Listed banks: Mosul Bank and IMEIB filed 2024 final accounts with the regulator (grade A, filing only); neither notice names an AML system. The Sumer and Asia Iraq Islamic home pages have no AML text. No bank annual report with an AML paragraph was read.
- Q3: no "Member Check", "MemberCheck", "NameScan" or "Name Scan" (or the Arabic forms) in the readable Iraqi bank text checked. Three scanned PDFs and two garbled PDFs could not be searched, so this is not a full census.
- Q4: file 18 has no record of Prototype. The overlap is goAML: file 18 records CapitalCompliance at Sumer and Pio-Tech at IMTB, and Prototype sells a "Go AML Reporting System". KIIB's AML supplier (Azentio) is not a Prototype product.
- Q5: NameScan's pages list no reseller, no partner programme, no Iraqi customer and no Prototype. It sells pay-per-search credits through a dashboard or API. Its 2024 coverage table lists Iraq. Its documents sit on a MemberCheck host, and its footer says it is "a member of the Neurocom group".
- Q6: no signing, training, exhibition, Central Bank of Iraq event or award involving Prototype and a bank was found.
- Gaps: no annual report found for Mosul, IMEIB, KIIB, Sumer, Asia Iraq Islamic, Trust International, National Islamic or Economy Bank. Misk and IDB have no reachable website. LinkedIn, job adverts and the CBI site were not searched.

## Findings

No. In 15 web searches (English and Arabic) and in the documents read, no bank, auditor, regulator, news outlet or event page names Prototype, Prototype IQ or بروتوتايب as a supplier. The queries are listed under Not found.

[INFERENCE] The silence is not proof. The search index covers few Iraqi bank documents, and five of the six regulator PDFs could not be read as text (see Not found).
