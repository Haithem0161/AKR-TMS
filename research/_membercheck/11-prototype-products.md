# MemberCheck notes 11 — Prototype for Information Technology: AML, screening and KYC products
Status: partial (2026-10-11)

## Summary
- Company: Prototype for Information Technology (Baghdad). Its homepage calls it "an Iraqi fintech and cybersecurity company". Everything here is the company's own description or a search snippet. No product was shown working, and no third party confirmed one. [PRIMARY]
- Sanctions family (Q1): four products, and no sanctions list is named on the cards. The brochure names OFAC only ("Real-time OFAC Compliance", p. 7). Name handling appears only as illustrations (translation, Latin spellings, an alias list). The method, languages and accuracy are not given. [PRIMARY]
- Own engine or partner (Q2): not settled. The homepage lists Namescan and Online AML as "Technology Partners", and some card wording matches theirs. No page says Prototype uses their data or matching. A partner feed for at least the lists looks plausible. [INFERENCE]
- AML AI (Q3): the cards and About text claim AI-based detection, transaction monitoring, risk scoring and regulatory reporting, under the name "AGIES AI for AML/CFT". The brochure's AML page (p. 7) describes pattern detection and OFAC screening, not transaction monitoring. No screen, demo or named bank confirms it. [PRIMARY]
- Go AML (Q4): the card claims suspicious-activity detection and reporting automation. Nothing says it builds files for any FIU portal. A 2022 Central Bank of Iraq item describes goAML e-reporting being introduced by Iraq's AML/CFT office. Nothing links that to Prototype. [SECONDARY]
- Other products (Q5, Q6): customer risk, eKYC, FATCA, credit bureau and fraud are one-line claims with bullets. The FATCA page calls the company the "FIRST IRS-APPROVED COMPANY", and the same seal appears on a PCI and SWIFT assessment page (p. 20). ProtoAudit and ProtoBot appear only as names in the About text, and nothing found says what they do. [PRIMARY]
- Bank connection and price (Q7): no deployment model (installed or hosted), no interface specification and no price figures. The services page shows price labels only ("Enterprise", "Custom", "Quote"). The homepage logo block lists about 30 bank and insurance names, seven with a product label. No bank confirms any of them. [PRIMARY]
- Evidence quality (Q8): weak. "Certified QSA", "IRS-approved", a "100%" compliance rate and "Trusted by leading financial institutions" have no supporting document on the pages read. The brochure has a poor text layer, "AI-generated content" labels and printed typos. [PRIMARY]
- Grades (Q9): every product grades C. None reaches A or B.
